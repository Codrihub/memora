# Memora name and logo

"Memora" and the Memora logo are used by Codrihub Tech to identify the
official project at https://github.com/Codrihub/memora. The code is open source
under the GNU AGPL (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE)); the name and
logo are not part of that license.

## You may

- Say that your project is "based on Memora" or "a fork of Memora".
- Use the name to refer to the official project, for example in articles,
  tutorials, comparisons or integrations.
- Keep the name and logo unchanged in a copy you run for yourself or your
  organization.

## Please don't

- Distribute a modified version, or offer one to others as a hosted service,
  under the name "Memora" or with the Memora logo. Give it your own name and
  say it is based on Memora.
- Suggest that your version, service or product is official, endorsed by or
  affiliated with Codrihub Tech when it is not.
